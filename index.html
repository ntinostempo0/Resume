About Me

I was born in Aigio, Greece, a small town near Patras. From an early age, I discovered my passion for athletics, beginning with football and gymnastics before moving on to basketball and tennis.

Music has always been an important part of my life. I play the piano and clarinet, and I hold degrees in Piano and Music Theory from the National Conservatory of Patras. I have performed with several rock, classical and philharmonic groups, although I especially enjoy composing my own music. My love of cinema also inspired my dream of composing music for films.

I enjoy travelling and discovering new places, cultures and cuisines. I also like reading, particularly classical literature and science books.

More recently, I have become interested in photography and videography. I mostly photograph nature, landscapes and animals, while astrophotography remains my ongoing work in progress. In the sections below, you can view some of my photographs and listen to my music.
